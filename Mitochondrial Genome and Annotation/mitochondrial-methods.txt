The Aedes togoi mitochondrial genome assembled with MitoHiFi was reverse-complemented and rotated to begin at trnI using Biopython (v1.88), to match the orientation mitochondrial genomes from Ae. japonicus, Ae. koreicus, and Ae. notoscriptus . Annotation combined de novo predictions from MITOS2 (v2.1.9), tRNAscan-SE (v2.0.13) and (ARAGORN v1.2.41) with reference-guided mapping against Ae. japonicus, Ae. koreicus, and Ae. notoscriptus mitogenomes using miniprot (v0.18) and BLASTn (v2.17.0). Discrepancies were resolved through MAFFT (v7.526) protein alignments under the invertebrate mitochondrial genetic code, retaining conserved terminal extensions and reference-supported start codons. Incomplete stop codons were retained where complete stops would overlap downstream tRNAs. tRNA boundaries followed MITOS2 predictions while rRNA boundaries were inferred from reference alignments and flanking tRNAs. Control-region tandem repeats were identified with Tandem Repeats Finder (v4.10.0), gene identities were calculated using Biopython global alignments, and the final feature table was validated with NCBI table2asn (v1.29.324).
